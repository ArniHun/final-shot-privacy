Privacy policy

iPhone test version · 30 October 2026

Contact

Tóth Arnold operates Final Shot. For privacy requests, contact irjarninak@gmail.com

Photos and recordings

Photos, videos and editable projects are stored on your device. Final Shot does not send your recordings to the operator. Copies saved to Photos may sync through iCloud according to your settings. Delete local projects and Photos copies separately.

Permissions

Camera, microphone and Photos permissions are used for capture, sound and access to your library. Voice control may use Apple’s online speech recognition. You can change permissions in iPhone Settings.

Ads and privacy choices

The test version uses Google AdMob test ads. The final free version available for download from the App Store may use AdMob ads. Google UMP retrieves privacy settings. The app requests ads only when UMP permits it. When available, open Ad privacy in the library to review your choices. Depending on settings and permissions, Google may process IP addresses, device identifiers, ad interactions and diagnostics to deliver and measure ads and prevent abuse. Google

Local settings and purchases

Settings, export counts and offer dates stay on your device until app data is deleted. Apple handles available purchases. Final Shot verifies transactions to determine ad-free access and does not request your card details. Apple

Your rights

Your email address and message are used to answer your request. You may request access, correction, deletion or restriction. Where applicable, you may object, request portability or withdraw consent. You may complain to the Hungarian data protection authority. NAIH
