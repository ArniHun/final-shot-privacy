Privacy Policy

Updated: 10 November 2026 · iOS app

Support and contact

Final Shot is an iPhone app for taking and editing photos and videos. If you have a question, encounter a problem, need help with a subscription or want to suggest an improvement, you can contact the developer.

Developer and contact: Tóth Arnold

Email: irjarninak@gmail.com

Reporting a problem

Please include, if possible, your iPhone model, iOS version, Final Shot app version and build number, a brief description of the issue and the steps needed to reproduce it. You may attach a screenshot if it does not reveal personal or confidential information. Please do not send passwords, payment card information or sensitive photos.

Subscriptions and purchases

In-app subscriptions and purchases are handled by Apple. You can review or manage active subscriptions on your iPhone in Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions. For technical or app-related subscription problems, contact the developer using the email address above. Final Shot does not ask for payment card details.

Photos and recordings

Photos, videos and editable projects are stored on your device. Final Shot does not send your recordings to the developer. Copies saved to the Photos app may be synced via iCloud depending on your settings. Local projects and copies in Photos can be deleted separately.

Permissions

The app uses camera, microphone and Photos permissions for capture, audio and access to your photo library. Voice control may use Apple's online speech recognition. You can change permissions in iPhone Settings.

Ads and privacy choices

The free version of Final Shot may display ads provided by Google AdMob. Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) manages the relevant privacy choices. The app requests ads only when the applicable UMP settings allow it. When available, you can review your choices through “Ad privacy” in the app's media library. Depending on your settings and permissions, Google may process IP addresses, device identifiers, ad interactions and diagnostic data to provide and measure ads and prevent abuse.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy?hl=en

Local settings and purchases

Settings, export counts and dates associated with offers remain on your device until the app's data is deleted. Apple processes available purchases. Final Shot checks transactions to determine eligibility for ad-free access and does not collect your payment card information.

Apple Privacy Policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

Your rights and privacy requests

Your email address and the content of your message are used to handle and respond to your request. You may request access, correction, deletion or restriction of personal data processing. Where applicable, you may object, request data portability or withdraw consent. You may lodge a complaint with the Hungarian data protection authority (NAIH).

Privacy contact: irjarninak@gmail.com

NAIH: https://naih.hu/
